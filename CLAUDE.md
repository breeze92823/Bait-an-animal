## The game
One hub with **five plots** side by side along the path (`PLOTS.xs`, 78 m apart): each has the same base, harpoon, canyon and Lane (10 detailed zones north of the canyon, see below). The player's is the middle one (x = 0, every `BASE`/`LANE` position is for it); the other four (`main.js` `otherPlots`) are built by `buildBase(scene, { x, vacant: true, boards: false })` (no upgrade signs, sell stall, buy boards, Expand Pen sign or ring upgrade pad) and `buildZones(scene, { ox, decor: true })` (hiding spots baked into the zone mesh, culled by x+z distance `LANE.decorView`, no fog control). Colliders of a copy are shifted by `propkit.colliderShift.x` during its build; anything a copy rebuilds later must run inside `withColliderShift(plotX, ...)` (Auto Machine / ring bought, `pen.setLevel`). **Multiplayer**: one room = this hub, 5 players, the server gives each a `plot` (0..4). Every client keeps ITS plot in the middle, so plots sit on a ring: another player shows at slot `wrap(theirPlot - myPlot) * 78` and their own-frame positions are mapped plot by plot (`remote.js` `mapX`). `remote.js` draws each other player (Bloxity character: `Legion.SDK.character.dress` from their outfit key, else the manual loader from their equipped ids; walk/jump from synced speed/vy; name tag; pets) and drives their plot's copy from the roster (`pen`: animals + pets, `mach`: machines, tanks, passes, ring, Expand Pen level, from `game.baseSnapshot()`; `hook`: the catch on their line) plus `fx` events (`presence.js` `sendFx`: pump, claim, purchases, harpoon arm/fire, caught, rep) replaying the same particles there. A plot nobody holds is the empty base. `net.js` `tickPresence` streams it all (`NET.moveEvery` / `NET.baseEvery`). Layout, seen from the south end facing north: open field (spawn) -> the pen (gate on its north side) -> an aisle between two machine pads (east: the manual machine, tanks along its east edge and buttons facing the aisle, upgrade signs at its north end; west: the Auto Machine pad, roped off with a wooden buy board (`BASE.autoMachine.forSale`) until bought, then three motor tanks + pipe manifold + console; the sign at its north end upgrades Auto Speed once it is owned) -> the yellow path running east-west along the north cliffs (sell stall, name sign, harpoon on it) -> the canyon (blue-panelled walls) north of the base -> the Lane's zones where the spear lands. Positions: `PATH`, `LANE`, `BASE` in config (base centred on x = 0). Loop: pump the bait machine (E / hold E / click it) -> XP levels the bait (Worm -> Cricket -> Beetle -> Firefly -> Golden Grub -> Star Snail -> Rainbow Slug, 5 levels each) -> Claim a bait (regrows in `BAITS.regrow` s, bag holds `BAITS.carryMax`) -> Attach Bait at the harpoon -> the bait flow (below) -> the catch jumps into the pen -> animals earn $/s (popups + clinks). Bait level shifts the rarity odds (`RARITIES[].k`), luck multiplies rarities above Common, 3% shiny (x3).
- Spend money on: sign upgrades (cash multiplier, bait XP per pump, +1 pen slot, Expand Pen (sign outside the pen's south fence: `UPGRADES.pen`, `S.up.pen`; each level moves the south fence `BASE.pen.grow` m south, `penSouth(level)`; base.js rebuilds + re-bakes the fence and its colliders (`base.pen.setLevel`), animals.js `setPenSouth` keeps them inside, `SPAWN.z` follows it and a player standing in the taken-in ground is set down at the sign), Auto Machine then Auto Speed; the Auto Machine's three tanks grow their own bait at `MACHINE.autoXpPerSecond`, state `S.auto`, claimed one by one), the two tank passes (Auto Click, 2X XP), HUD passes (x2 Cash, x2 XP), the Shop (Store button: limited -50% Starter Pack `STORE.pack` = Jellyfish + Meadow Pony into the pen + all potions 30 min, bought once on a repeating timer; Upgrade Bait `STORE.baitSkip` = machine jumps to the next bait kind; passes 2x Luck / 2x Cash / 2X XP / Autoclicker; potions; Lucky Chick; Auto Machine), the timed OP Mammoth pet offer; the Dino Buddy pet is free. Sell stall: sell baits / animals. Offline Cash: animals earn `OFFLINE.rate` of their income while you are away (save timestamp `lastSeen`); it waits as a pile at `BASE.offline` until walked into / collected. Play Boost: +5% cash every 3 min played this session.
- Training ring (Strength), `BASE.gym` west of the Auto Machine pad near its +1 Slot sign: a wooden buy board (`GYM.price`, cheap) unlocks once an animal is in the pen; then E in the ring = train. The player walks to the centre (`player.lock`), squats, picks the weights off the mat and lifts on a loop; every rep adds `game.gymPerRep()` to `S.gym.strength` and 1 training XP (`S.gym.lv`, +`GYM.lvBonus` per level). The blue pad upgrades the equipment level `S.gym.level` (more strength per rep; dumbbell curls -> barbell overhead press at 5 -> front squats at 15, `GYM.moves`). Cancel button or any move input finishes the rep and sets the weights down. Strength also sets the harpoon's distance (bait flow).

- **Economy pacing** (rebalanced 2026-11-19; re-run `node tools/econ-sim.mjs sum` after changing any number, and keep its defaults in step with config): target = a typical player reaches Angelic in ~5 h and maxes every upgrade in ~10 h of active play (casual ~5 h / 11 h, tryhard ~3.3 h / 9 h). Zone income x4 per zone (`ZONES[].mult`); Strength is the zone gate (`HARPOON.reach.k` 42: each zone needs ~3x the Strength of the last, so the training ring's money-bought levels and AFK reps both matter); bait stops at Rainbow Slug Lv 5 (`BAITS.maxLevel`, tank label shows MAX); low rarities keep a `floor` share of catches; shiny chance capped (`SHINY.cap`); potions and Next Shot cost at least N seconds of pen income (`game.potionPrice`, `incomePrice`). `load()` clamps old saves (bait level, upgrade levels) to the new caps. The harpoon reach is not capped by the Lane: the aim screen's **Target** picker (`game.aimZones()`) offers every zone below the farthest one the longest shot reaches (all ten once the reach passes the Lane's end), and a targeted shot always lands in that zone (power only sets where in it), so early zones stay catchable for the Index.
- **Bait flow** (`baitFlow.js/.css`, reference-driven): Attach Bait mounts the player on the harpoon (`player.locked`, camera behind it); a 5-segment power meter sweeps (`HARPOON.meter`: fill = x1..x2 luck, rating Weak/Good/Great/Perfect), Bait (button / Space / E) fires, Cancel (button / Backspace / X) climbs off; "Next Shot 100x Luck" is bought with money (`NEXT_SHOT`, `S.nextShot`). Flight: chase cam, glowing trail, "Harpoon Distance: Nm" over a 10-zone bar, luck multiplier grows with distance (`HARPOON.distLuck`). Distance = Strength (`S.gym.strength`, the training ring) on a log scale (`HARPOON.reach`, `game.maxDistance()`). The spear always lands in front of a **hiding spot** (themed bush/rock/coral... per zone, `zones.js` `nearestSpot`), which glows white-hot while its luck counts up (`HARPOON.bush` bands, `HARPOON.charge`), shakes and rustles, then the catch bursts out ("1 in N", "HOOKED!"; rolled by `game.rollShot(p, luck, zone)`, roll luck = shot luck ^ `HARPOON.luckPower`). Reel: it reels itself, clicks/taps/Space pull faster, tugs = "Pull Harder!" (`HARPOON.reel`), dragged up the Lane, glows white at the harpoon, `game.deliverCatch` -> pen. Sounds: `audio/game.js` (mount, meterTick, rating, flightWind/luckRiser loops, luckTick, rustle, hooked, reelClick, tug, scuff, caught). The one point light in the flow stays 1.5 m+ off surfaces: half-float HDR + bloom turns overflow/NaN into a black screen.
- **Lane zones** (`zones.js`, `ZONES`/`zoneStart`/`zoneAt` in config): Meadow (canyon + 70 m) then Coral, Snow, Desert, Crystal, Jungle, Golden, Ancient, Hell, Angelic, `LANE.zoneLength` each, each with ground, cliffs (no gateway arches/signs: removed on request), themed props, emissive "lights", GPU particles and hiding spots; each zone baked to one group, hidden past `LANE.viewDistance` from the camera (fog `FOG` matches; tighter while the camera is out in the Lane). `renderZoneThumbs` snapshots each zone at boot for the Index (`THUMBS.zone`).
- **Animals**: 63 species in `ANIMALS` (config `ROSTER`), 6-8 per zone, 8 rarities (`RARITIES` incl. Celestial, Secret; `tile` colours for the Index); income = rarity base * f * zone mult. Sizes: config `SIZE` = each species' height in m (player 1.8 m; critters ~1-1.5, big mammals 3-5, giants like T-Rex/Brachiosaurus/dragons 6-9.5); `buildAnimal` scales the base model to it (`rig.scale`, footprint `rig.radius` for pen margins/spacing). Models: `animals.js` base models (the 12 originals + crab, fish, turtle, frog, snake, jelly, bird, horse, longneck, ape, cat) recoloured per species by `look` (tint, gold, glow eyes, extras: horn(s), halo, wings, batwings, flames, crystals, hump, sail, plates, spikes, crown, stripes, mane, longnose, fin).
- **Pet Index** (`windows.js` + `indexWindow.css`, panel skin): per-zone sections (zone picture, rarity tiles, "Collect all Pets for +20% Cash" = `INDEX.zoneBonus` into `cashMult`), selected-animal panel with a one-time Claim reward (income * `INDEX.claimSeconds`, `S.claimed`), CLAIM ALL.

## Stack
Three.js (vanilla) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures, voxel boxes, synthesised audio); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka.
- `npm run dev` / `npm run build`. Preview server: `bait-dev` in the session-root `.claude/launch.json` (port 5291, `VITE_DEV_MODE=true` skips the Bloxity SDK/CDN). Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` uploads `dist/`.
- All tunables are in `src/config.js`: world (`HUB`, `LANE`, `BASE` positions, `SPAWN`), rules (`BAITS`, `MACHINE`, `RARITIES`, `ANIMALS`, `SHINY`, `UPGRADES`, `PASSES`, `PETS`, `OFFER`, `STORE`, `PLAY_BOOST`, `HARPOON`, `INTERACT`, `SAVE`), `AUDIO`, `JUICE`, plus the template's look/physics/camera/rig sections. `CAMERA.pointerLock: false` = free cursor, left/right drag orbits.
- All Bloxity SDK calls go through `src/bloxity.js` and must never throw. Boxity draws its toolbar + chat in the top-left corner: the HUD and windows stay out of those rectangles (`bloxity.onReservedAreas` -> `hud.setReserved`, `uiWindow.setWindowReserved`); on short landscape screens the Store/Pets/Index column becomes a row beside the toolbar.
- **Boxity platform features** (docs.bloxity.io; every one listed with its file in `BLOXITY_CODE_METADATA.md`, which also records `canDie: false`, `hasScore: true`, `rewards: true` and what is not applicable: Gems by design, the death screen, CrazyGames). Server identity: the join and `identify` send `auth.getToken()`; the server verifies it and ignores any client id (dev mode without the SDK has no token = a guest, saves in this browser). `?roomId=` (Open Rooms Join / invites) is joined once at launch through the room's pod (`locateRoom`), falling back to matchmaking; friend toasts `playerInRoom` / `playerJoined` (remote.js, `net.initialSids`). Leaderboards `LEADERBOARDS` are only declared here (trophy button); the server reports the scores. Emotes: `registerFeature('emotes')`, `emotes.js` plays the catalogue's clips on the rig (`EmotePlayer`, local + remote via `fx emote/emoteStop`, stops on movement; dev console `__game.emote(id)` after `loadEmotes('https://api.bloxity.io')`). Chat: `chat.onMessage` -> `chat.attachBubble` over the sender (remotes matched by the verified `uid`). Ads: breaks pause the loop + `setAdMuted`; screen ads `panel` while a window is open (`uiWindow.onWindowChange`), `gameplay` (no banners) otherwise; rewarded HUD card (`hud-pass ad`, only when `ads.isAvailable()`) = `AD_REWARD` 2x Cash 10 min after the server's `adReward` check (`game.grantPotion`).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
